# Results catalog — experiment `probe-design_v2`

The curated design keepers for the v2 panel. All are regenerated by `../generate/build.js` from the
committed inputs in `../generate/inputs/` (see `../config.snapshot.yaml`). Probe FASTAs are *not* here —
they are machine-generated by `../matlab/EP_ORCA_v2_probes.m` into `../data/` (gitignored) and recorded
at the bottom of this file. Visibility: **private** (see `../MANIFEST.md`).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

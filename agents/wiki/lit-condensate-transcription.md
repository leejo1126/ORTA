BRD4 and MED1 form liquid-like nuclear puncta (condensates) at super-enhancers; their
intrinsically disordered regions phase-separate, and the puncta are dissolved by
1,6-hexanediol — linking phase separation to gene control (Sabari 2018). Mediator and
Pol II clusters associate in **transcription-dependent** condensates (Cho 2018).

**Implication for detection.** Brd4 is not a single population: expect a **heavy-tailed
size distribution** — very many small puncta plus a few larger super-enhancer condensates.
A detector tuned only to small spots will miss the large condensates and vice-versa; the
size gate must admit both, and over-splitting large condensates is a real failure mode.
